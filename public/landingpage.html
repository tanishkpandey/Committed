The problem

Why do good habits fade?

Most habit tracking fails because conventional tools ignore human psychology.

Out of sight, out of mind

When your daily effort isn't immediately visible, skipping one Tuesday feels harmless. Then Wednesday slips. Committed turns progress into living emerald contribution walls you actually want to protect.

The all or nothing trap

Missing a single day makes people feel defeated, causing them to abandon the entire routine. Committed focuses on long-term consistency rates and rolling averages, so one rough day never erases your momentum.

No sense of reward

Boring checklists feel like unpaid chores. Without positive feedback, willpower dries up fast. Committed pairs tactile 1-tap check-ins with RPG leveling, XP bonuses, and rare badges that make showing up fun.
