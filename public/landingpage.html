The problem

Why do good habits fade?

Most habit apps fail for the same three simple reasons.

Out of sight

When you can't see your daily effort, it is easy to skip today. Then tomorrow. Then you stop.

All or nothing

Missing one day makes people feel like they failed, so they give up on the whole habit.

No sense of reward

Plain checklists feel like chores. Without visible progress, habits feel empty and slow.
